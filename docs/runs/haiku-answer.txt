## Summary

**Revenue dropped 1.3% from June to July 2026** ($324.0M → $319.9M, a decline of ~$4.1M). The root cause is a **significant drop in average order value in the paid search channel**.

### Key Findings:

**1. Paid Search Channel Collapsed** (q4)
- Paid search revenue dropped **$12.2M** (15% decline)
- This single channel accounts for the entire revenue drop—other channels actually grew:
  - Organic: +$2.8M
  - Partner: +$2.7M  
  - Email: +$2.7M

**2. The Problem: Average Order Value, Not Volume** (q5)
- Paid search orders increased from 540K to 558K (+3.3%)
- BUT average order value dropped **18%**: from $149.90 → $123.15
- This $26.75 AOV decline across 558K orders = $12.2M revenue loss

### Interpretation:

Despite driving more orders in July, paid search is converting lower-value customers or selling lower-value products. This could indicate:
- Traffic quality degradation (attracting lower-intent users)
- Changed keyword mix or bidding strategy
- Promotional pricing or discounts applied
- Seasonal product mix shift to lower-priced items
